Water Saving Tips

Turn Off the Tap

Turn off the tap while brushing teeth or soaping up in the shower. This can save up to 8 gallons per day.

Use a Bucket

Use a bucket in the shower to collect water for plants or flushing the toilet.

Collect Rainwater

Install a rain barrel to collect rainwater for outdoor use and gardening.

Fix Leaks

Fix leaky faucets and toilets immediately. A dripping faucet can waste 3,000 gallons per year.
